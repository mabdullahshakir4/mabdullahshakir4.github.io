Why this view matters

ACOS is useful, but it is not a complete business metric. A strong Amazon performance review needs to consider whether paid traffic is contributing to profitable growth, how conversion is moving, how much demand is becoming organic, and whether account economics support further investment.

What I evaluate

  • Sales and profit trend
  • Orders and units
  • Sessions and conversion
  • Organic orders and units
  • PPC contribution to total sales
  • Promotions and refunds
  • Movement across consecutive monthly periods

Decision framework

I generally compare 7D, 14D, 30D, L7D, L30D and lifetime perspectives. The purpose is to avoid making decisions from one isolated number and instead understand direction, efficiency and commercial impact.
